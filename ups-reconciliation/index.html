📊By customer

Code Name Rate level Orders Packages Billed to customer Charged by UPS Profit Margin Recomputed vs UPS

Profit is what the customer paid less what UPS charged. Recomputed is what the customer’s own rate level says the shipments should have cost; set against what UPS charged, it shows where the invoice differs from the agreement. Charged by UPS excludes account-level charges: they carry no tracking number and belong to no order, so whether they count is your accounting call — the dashboard treats them the same way.

🔍By charge

Every charge on the matched shipments, priced two ways: what this customer’s rate level says it should be, and what UPS actually billed. The difference is where the invoice departs from the agreement — sorted with the largest gap first.
